# NURS 497 Nursing Capstone - interactives plan

Source: `_course-source.md`, extracted from
`ExportFile_NURS497-QM-MASTER_20261014021528.zip` (14 October 2026).
That package replaces the September practicum export. Do not rebuild from the September file.

Each module has one main interactive and one persistent checklist. Paste the two HTML files.
Do not paste `.data.js`.

## Modules

1. Facing the Challenges of Today's Workplace. Process chain of Benner's stages, then a unit issue, stage, and one mentor goal. Discussion (MLO 1.3). Authentic Assignment (MLO 1.2-1.3). Quiz (MLO 1.1-1.2).
2. Evidence-Based Practice and Technology in Health Care. Sort tools that guide a decision from tools that only store the record, then test a study for the bibliography and an EHR audit sentence. Discussion (MLO 2.2). Authentic Assignment (MLO 2.1-2.3). Quiz (MLO 2.1, 2.3).
3. Client Safety, Quality Initiatives, and Workplace Health and Safety. Red-flag choices, a four-bin cause sort, and a SMART aim. Discussion (MLO 3.2). Authentic Assignment (MLO 3.1, 3.3). Quiz (MLO 3.1-3.2).
4. Nursing for the Future: Research, Informatics, and Telehealth. Match a tool to the outcome it changes, then place the alert and name the metric. Discussion (MLO 4.3). Authentic Assignment (MLO 4.2-4.3). Quiz (MLO 4.1-4.3).
5. Working With Others In Healthcare. Stakeholder hot seat using SBAR, a huddle, and CUS. Discussion (MLO 5.3). Authentic Assignment (MLO 5.1-5.2). Quiz (MLO 5.1-5.2).
6. Emotional Intelligence, Accountability, and Authority. Matrix of accountability against authority, then a bad-news choice and a policy appraisal. Discussion (MLO 6.3). Authentic Assignment (MLO 6.2-6.3). Quiz (MLO 6.1-6.2).
7. Political Processes in Nursing and Health Care Reform. Branch on a board, a bill, or an internal policy, then pick the move that reaches that door. Discussion (MLO 7.3). Authentic Assignment (MLO 7.1-7.3). Quiz (MLO 7.1-7.2).
8. Making Professional Goals a Reality. Timeline of career goals and a match of evidence to the three e-portfolio sections. Discussion (MLO 8.2). Capstone Project (MLO 8.1-8.3). Quiz (MLO 8.1, 8.3).

Checklist lanes: Instructional Materials, Activities, Assignments and Assessments. Discussions sit under Activities. Assignments and quizzes sit under Assignments and Assessments.
