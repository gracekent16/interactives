Who Owns It, and Who Can Change It?

Place each demand on the grid of accountability and authority, then choose how emotional intelligence changes a hard conversation.

MLO 6.1-6.3

Module 6 separates two things that get blurred on a unit. Accountability is owning the outcome of your practice. Authority is the power to change the rule, the budget, or the policy. Emotional intelligence is how you stay useful when the conversation is hard.
